Scientific Name:

Living Particle

Iris Wang, Fall 2026

Family:

Proton, neutron, electron; our family; Planets, galaxy, nebula(all celestial body).

Habitat:

Cell, universe, human society, or our home.

Appearance:

Changing between a dark circle (symbolizing atom), a planet, and a human face.

Key Characteristics:

  • Keep changing appearance.
  • Actively react to users’ action.
  • Actively make impact to environment.

WE is a living particle that exists across different scales, from atoms and celestial bodies to human life. Its appearance constantly changes, and it actively responds to both its environment and the actions of the user.
When the user controls the creature with the mouse, it can move and collide with other circles in the environment. After the collision, the white and blue circles will react by shaking, changing color, or shifting their original positions. This interaction will show how one action can affect not only the creature itself but also the environment around it.
